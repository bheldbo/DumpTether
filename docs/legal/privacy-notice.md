# DumpTether Privacy Notice

Version: `2026-08-06`

> Production note: this document is a deployment template. The configured
> operator must add its complete public identity and contact details and verify
> processor, transfer, and retention statements before public registration.

## Controller

The controller is the operator identified by `Legal:OperatorName`. Privacy
requests go to `Legal:PrivacyContactEmail`.

## Data processed

DumpTether processes:

- account email, display name, confirmation state, and external login identity;
- session and security metadata such as device label, user-agent summary, and
  protected network metadata;
- boards, tasks, notes, structured fields, templates, categories, sharing
  records, and settings supplied by users;
- limited operational logs needed to secure and run the service.

Passwords and session tokens are not stored in raw form.

## Purposes and legal bases

Data is processed to provide and secure the requested service, fulfil the
account agreement, synchronize and share content according to user actions,
prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. The expected legal bases are
performance of a contract, legitimate interests in service security and abuse
prevention, and legal obligations where applicable. DumpTether does not sell
personal data.

## Processors and transfers

The hosted deployment may use its VPS/hosting provider, Cloudflare, Brevo, and
Microsoft when Microsoft sign-in is selected. The operator must maintain the
applicable processor agreements and assess international transfer safeguards.

## Retention

Content is retained while the account or workspace needs it. Session and
archive retention follow deployment settings. Operational logs and backups may
have separate, limited retention periods. The operator must document actual
backup and log schedules; configuration defaults are not a complete retention
policy.

## Rights

Depending on the circumstances, a person may request access, correction,
deletion, restriction, objection, or portability. A person may also complain
to the Danish Data Protection Agency. Requests should be sent to the configured
privacy contact.

Account holders can schedule self-service deletion from Account settings. The
request has a 48-hour cancellation period and a reminder is sent about 24 hours
before deletion. Deletion of an account that owns shared boards is blocked until
the owner removes those sharing relationships. A portable self-service export
is not complete yet; the operator must provide a documented manual access and
export process until it is.

## Cookies

The current service uses strictly necessary session and CSRF-protection
cookies. It does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.
